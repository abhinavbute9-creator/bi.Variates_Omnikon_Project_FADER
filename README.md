# bi.Variates_Omnikon_Project
The repository is primary made to work on the project of problem statement 
ID: Omni_Transport_9
Name: Reducing Fatigue Related Trucking Accidents.
The team "bi.Variates" consisting of two members: Abhinav Bute and Atharva Kale are the primary host of this repoistory which will substitute each form of files and folders required for the project.
Our proposed project is named "FADER" i.e. Fatigue Awareness and Driver Engagement Routing. As while long intervals of travelling drivers, irrespective of whatever the vehicle they are travelling with
the monotonous form of their journey especially during the straight highways with minimal traffic or external actions causes them to lose control of their focus causing short intervals of sleep which could
be small, but highly risky enough for life threatening consequences. To deal with this, our team will make an application whose primarily work will be to interact with the driver while travelling beholding
the risk of any form of sleepiness while travelling, commanding and guiding as per the input and live weather features (via API) giving live coverage and constantly helping in entirety of travelling, till 
driver will reach the destination safely.
